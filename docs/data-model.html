S1KHFELP: one sellable BOM, one manufacturing policy, variable production quantities

The costing configuration describes the complete product being priced. The manufacturing configuration describes the standard way its parts are made. A production plan then chooses how much of each part to make in this run.

Model CodeS1KHFELP identifies the sellable configuration.
Costing Configuration RevisionSelects the complete BOM and exact part revisions used for pricing.
Manufacturing Configuration RevisionDefines standard routing, issue route, batch policy, and source department.
Production PlanCreates an actual production run and checks available/reserved stock.
Production BatchesRelease only the required part batches and their Store/Tool Shop/CNC work.
Drum Assembly80 NosCommon across products; planned for this and future runs.
Chassis32 NosAligned to the current finished-product requirement.
Support64 NosTwo batches together because its manufacturing lead line is longer.
Key rule: 80/32/64 are values on ProductionPlanPartLine. They do not revise S1KHFELP’s costing BOM, the common Drum Assembly, or the standard manufacturing configuration. The next plan considers surplus available and reserved quantities before proposing another Support or Drum batch.

What creates a new master, revision, observation, or cost run?

ODS row movesNew SourceLineObservation; same Line Master and revision when semantic values are unchanged.
Quantity, weight, formula, material, or process changesNew immutable LineRevision under the same LineMaster.
New physical/business requirementNew LineMaster and its first revision.
Material price changesNew RateObservation and, after approval, ApprovedRate; no line revision.
Model BOM changesNew CostingConfigurationRevision; shared Part and Line masters remain unchanged.
Standard routing or batch policy changesNew ManufacturingConfigurationRevision or part policy revision.
Different quantities for an actual runNew/revised ProductionPlan and plan part lines; no master/configuration change.
Recalculate with unchanged definitionsNew CostRun pinned to exact revisions and rate set.
