Confirmed design decisions

  • New Safari Manufacturing Grist document; preserve Costing-New.
  • One file belongs to one Model; one Model can have many files; one Code has one active file.
  • Costing, manufacturing, and spares configurations are separate and versioned.
  • The explorer-selected ODS workbook is the default costing authority for its assigned Model Codes; other folder workbooks may be scenario samples.
  • Milestone 2 extracts all RawSteel rows and the available SteelRateLog dump; parity follows the selected workbook's formula range and ₹2/kg safety margin. An unused out-of-range lookup is a consistency warning, not a blocker.
  • ODS revisions are compared semantically, then reconciled with Safari Manufacturing; a file change alone is not an issue.
  • Future costing policy may use a ₹2/kg default safety margin with per-material overrides; unloading and fabrication may have product-specific overrides.
  • New costing output rounds each active MCL line's grand total upward to a whole rupee before summing.
  • Latest Rate uses the final matching row in the current source snapshot even if entry dates regress or tie. Max Rate uses only non-zero rates available in that snapshot; archived Grist history is excluded unless included in the snapshot.
  • Future-looking parsed dates are informational because Google Sheets and LibreOffice can interpret dates differently. Confirmed invalid rate entries block the displayed rate across consuming workbooks until resolved; the UI must hide the rate and highlight the issue.
  • Business parity tolerance is ±₹100 at the overall costing-total level; exact line and component differences remain visible.
  • Observed purchase rates and approved costing rates remain separate domains.
  • Tool Shop source and Stores issue route can coexist without double costing.
  • UI and services depend on repositories, allowing a later PostgreSQL adapter.

Inputs still needed

  • Current blocking rate checks cover missing, nonnumeric, or nonpositive prices and missing/unparseable dates. Future-looking dates alone are informational; define any additional ambiguous invalid cases before expanding the blocker policy.
  • Workbook preview can refresh local ODS links through LibreOffice on a disposable copy, preserving the source and linked files. Durable rate issues are created by an explicit reconciliation scan. Carry semantic ODS-to-Safari changes through governed review; upstream Google Sheets dump acquisition remains future automation.
  • There is no fixed dump refresh cadence today; scripted acquisition and any freshness threshold are future work. Transport is generally shared but may vary by supplier; internal unloading and fabrication rates are universal today with product overrides planned.
  • The pilot summary cache includes two historical In Use = No lines; the agreed semantic total excludes them. This legacy formula/cache mismatch remains documented for reconciliation.
